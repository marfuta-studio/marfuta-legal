Marfuta Listing Builder: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

What this app is

Marfuta Listing Builder is a private desktop application used by the owners of the MarfutaStudio shop on Etsy to prepare product listings and to read the shop's own statistics. It is not offered to the public, has no accounts of its own and has no server. It runs only on the owners' computers.

What we access on Pinterest

Only with the explicit authorization of the Pinterest account holder (OAuth), the app may:

  • read the account holder's own Pins, boards and analytics;
  • create a Pin on the account holder's own boards, only after the account holder has reviewed that exact Pin and chosen to publish it.

The app never publishes, saves, follows or comments automatically.

What we store

Access tokens and API credentials are kept encrypted on the owner's own computer and are never sent anywhere else. Apart from analytics about the account holder's own account, Pinterest data is not stored: it is requested from the Pinterest API when needed and shown on screen.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or transfer Pinterest data to third parties.
  • We do not use Pinterest data to train, fine-tune or improve any artificial intelligence or machine learning model.
  • We do not collect data about other Pinterest users, and we do not combine Pinterest data with personal data.
  • We do not use Pinterest data for advertising outside Pinterest.

Your choices

The account holder can disconnect the app at any time, in the app or in the Pinterest account settings (Settings, Apps). On disconnection the stored token is deleted from the computer. Any stored Pinterest account analytics can be deleted on request.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/marfuta-studio/marfuta-legal/issues.
